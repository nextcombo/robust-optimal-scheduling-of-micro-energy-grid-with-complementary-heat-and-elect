# Robust Optimal Scheduling of Micro-Energy Grid with Complementary Heat and Electricity, Based on Multi-energy Conservation

Abstract: With the adjustment of energy structure, micro-energy networks integrating wind/solar and other renewable energy inputs with complementary outputs of heat, cold, and electricity have been gradually developed. How to coordinate the dispatching of micro-energy networks within the grid or island mode has become a current research hotspot. A coordination dispatch model for micro-energy networks with multi-energy complementarity in island/grid modes is established. Utilizing the thermal inertia of heating/cooling systems and the flexibility of thermal/electrical loads, the "storage" function of heating/cooling systems is leveraged to realize bidirectional interconnection between electric and gas networks through P2G (Power-to-Gas) devices. The model employs robust linear optimization theory to transform the stochastic optimization model into a deterministic one, achieving a compromise between economic efficiency and robustness. Case simulations verify the optimization role of temperature load storage characteristics in flexible dispatching of micro-energy networks and the coordination role of robustness indicators in optimizing results.

This paper considers the thermal inertia of heating/cooling systems and adopts a flexible heating/cooling control method, which plays the "energy storage" function of thermal/cold temperature loads. The introduction of PH2G devices realizes closed-loop interconnection between the electrical and gas networks. A coordination dispatch model for micro-energy networks with multi-energy complementarity in island/grid modes is established. Robust linear optimization theory is employed to solve the stochastic optimization model, overcoming the dependence on the probability distribution of random variables. Simulation results show that the "energy storage" characteristic of temperature load can effectively enhance the flexibility and robustness of micro-energy network coordination dispatch, as well as facilitate the control of robustness indicators.
